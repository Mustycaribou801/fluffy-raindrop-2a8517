Historical Forecasting Performance

Every published Mustycaribou forecast, scored against the certified result. This page brings the 2024 and 2026 track records together in one place: how many winners each forecast called correctly, by election and by year.

A forecast counts as correct when the predicted winner of a state, seat or race won. Every total and percentage below is calculated from the individual election records. The full state-by-state audits are in the 2024 Election Archive and the 2026 Election Archive.

US Senate Forecasting Record

The Senate is the only office forecast in both cycles. These figures combine the 2024 and 2026 Senate forecasts.

The 2024 records cover the final presidential forecast for all 50 states, the 34 Senate races and all 435 House seats. The 2026 records cover the 35 Senate races and 36 governor's races in the final forecast published on 6 July 2026. A race is scored only once its winner has been called or certified. Accuracy is correct calls divided by total races forecast.
