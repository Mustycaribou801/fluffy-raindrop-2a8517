      Forecasts are the final pre-election ratings and margins published on 6 July 2026, reproduced unchanged. A race is scored only once a winner has been called by a decision desk or certified. A candidate who is only leading is never treated as the winner. Competitive races are defined, as on the 2024 Track Record, as races with an actual margin under 6 points. Margin error and bias are calculated only for races with a published numerical margin forecast. Bias is the average of predicted minus actual Democratic margin: positive means the forecasts were too Democratic, negative too Republican. No 2026 House forecasts were published, so none are scored here.
