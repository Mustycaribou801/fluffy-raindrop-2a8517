    Methodology & data sources

    How this review is calculated

    Predictions

    • Each prediction is the final pre-election forecast published on this site (). It has not been changed since.
    • Predicted party comes from the rating: Safe, Likely and Lean D are Democratic calls, and the R versions are Republican calls. A Toss-up uses the sign of the projected margin, and a Toss-up with no margin isn't scored.
    • Senate forecasts had a rating and a projected margin. Governor forecasts had a rating only.

    Results

    • Election-night figures come from the NBC News results pages, which publish vote data from The Associated Press. Race calls are the NBC News Decision Desk's projections. Each race links to its source page and records when it was read. They will be replaced with certified figures from state election offices as those are published. Nothing is estimated or filled in.
    • Called: a winner declared by a decision desk. Certified: official state result. Uncalled: votes reported but no winner declared; the current margin is shown. Runoff: race goes to a second round; the first-round leader isn't treated as the winner. Pending: no result entered.
    • Only called and certified races count towards accuracy. A candidate who is leading is never treated as the winner.
    • Election-night shares are unofficial and change as counting continues, especially where a large share of the expected vote is still outstanding.

    Statistics

    • Margins are the winner's lead over the runner-up in percentage points, signed: D+ is a Democratic lead, R+ a Republican lead.
    • Actual margin = first-place share − second-place share, from the published shares (rounded to 0.1 pt). Where the main challenger was an independent (for example Nebraska, Idaho and Montana in the Senate), the margin is against that candidate.
    • Margin error = |predicted signed margin − actual signed margin|, where a Republican lead counts as negative. It's calculated only for called Senate races.
    • Winner accuracy = correct calls ÷ counted races. Pending races are left out and never treated as hits or misses.
    • Rating tiers: Safe (SD/SR), Likely (LD/LR), Lean (TD/TR) and Toss-up (TU).
    • Every figure on this page is recalculated in the browser from the race data whenever the page loads.

    Figures on this page are self-reported and archived for transparency. The 2024 review is on the Track Record page.
