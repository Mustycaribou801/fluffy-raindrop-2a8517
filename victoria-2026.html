This is an independent, seat-by-seat forecast of the Victorian Legislative Assembly. Each of the 88 electorates has been called individually, informed by public polling, past results and electoral analysis of local factors.

It is a judgement-based forecast, not a statistical or Monte Carlo model, so it does not publish win probabilities. The seat totals below are simply the sum of the individual electorate calls.
